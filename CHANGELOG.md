# Changelog

All notable changes to AniScroll appear here. Most recent first.

## [v0.0.1] — Public Beta (2026-06-01)

Welcome to AniScroll — a fast, clean place to watch anime, with no ads and no
tracking getting in your way. Sign in with your AniList account to sync your
list and keep your watch progress across devices, or just dive straight in.

This is our first public beta, so it's still rough around the edges — you'll
likely run into bugs, broken episodes or the odd glitch. If something doesn't
work, please use the report button so we can fix it quickly. Your feedback is
what shapes the app from here.

Thanks a lot for being here this early. While we're in beta the app stays on
the `0.x` line, starting at v0.0.1.
